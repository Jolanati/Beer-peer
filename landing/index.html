Chapter 1 · The Problem

You know what you're eating.
But what do you drink with it?

You're at a restaurant. The wine list has forty options and none of them mean anything to you. You pick something vaguely familiar, hope for the best, and move on.

The situation

Wine pairing feels like a skill you either have — or don't.

Most people don't feel confident choosing wine to match food. The knowledge gap is real, and it's intimidating.

Current options

Ask the sommelier. Google it. Or just order the house wine.

None of these start from what actually matters — what the food tastes like, right now, on your plate.

Our idea

What if the only thing you needed was a photo?

Wine & Dine removes the gap entirely. Photograph your dish. The system does the rest — no wine knowledge required.
